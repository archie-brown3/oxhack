# Doomsday Clock 2026 statement (Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists)

Source: https://thebulletin.org/doomsday-clock/2026-statement/ (summarised 24 Oct 2026).

- **Setting: 85 seconds to midnight**, "the closest it has ever been to catastrophe".
  2025 was 89 seconds (from memory, not on the page), so 4 seconds closer.
- The clock is a single expert judgement by the Science and Security Board, not a model.
  It has no stated probability, horizon or calibration record.

## Risk domains

- **Nuclear:** Russia–Ukraine nuclear allusions; India–Pakistan drone and missile strikes (May
  2025); US and Israeli strikes on Iranian nuclear sites (June 2025). Arms race: more Chinese
  warheads, US "Golden Dome" missile defence with space interceptors. New START expiring;
  possible resumption of US explosive testing.
- **Climate:** CO₂ at 150% of preindustrial. 2024 warmest in the 175-year record, 2025 similar.
  Record sea level. 60,000+ heat deaths in Europe; Congo Basin floods displaced 350,000;
  SE Brazil rain displaced 500,000+. Summits did not push fossil-fuel phase-out.
- **Biological:** mirror-life warning (Dec 2024, no international plan); AI-designed pathogens;
  weakened bioweapons norms; erosion of US public health capacity.
- **Disruptive tech / AI:** LLM hallucination; AI across US, Russian and Chinese militaries;
  US AI safety executive order revoked; AI-amplified disinformation.
- **Cross-cutting:** nationalist autocracy as a "threat accelerant".

## Recommended actions

US–Russia arms talks; no destabilising missile defence; keep the testing moratorium; prevent
mirror life; curb AI-enabled bio threats; US incentives to cut fossil fuels; US–Russia–China
dialogue on military AI, especially nuclear command and control.
