A turning-point card: an oxblood band, a serif headline and two lines. It closes itself after 4 s.

- **Provide:** the quarter, a headline as a statement, two lines of copy, the shift in months, and its direction and label.
- Use it only for turning points: a hidden move revealed, a treaty signed, or a new world-alarm stage. Routine news goes in the ticker.
- The `timer` hairline counts the 4 s down. Hovering pauses it. With reduced motion, the card stays until dismissed.
- **Don't:** add an OK button or block play.
