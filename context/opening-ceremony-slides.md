## Slide 28

```text
What We’re Looking For
 ●    Projects that feel fast - snappy UX, zero unnecessary friction.
 ●    Projects that solve a real problem. Not a demo. Something you’d
      actually open tomorrow.
 ●    Projects that are thoughtful. Clean code, smart architecture, good
      decisions.

Prompts to Get You Started
  •   What slows you down when you’re coding? What if that friction
      disappeared?
  •   What’s broken about collaborating on code with teammates or AI?
  •   What would you build if latency wasn’t a constraint?
  •   What context do you lose every time you switch tasks — and how do
      you get it back?

```

## Slide 29

```text
How You Win
Speed & Performance (30%) — Does it feel fast? Is the UX snappy and
responsive?
Technical Implementation (25%) — Clean code, smart architecture, good
use of collaboration features.
Innovation (25%) — Fresh idea. Real problem. New angle.
Polish & Usability (20%) — Would you actually use this? Does it delight?

```
