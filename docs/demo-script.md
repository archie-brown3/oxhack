0:00 World, today. "This clock reads prediction markets. Midnight is not a prophecy. It is the date those markets put a 1-in-10 chance of an AI catastrophe. The hand is the real forecast. The number of traders is on the Why sheet, and the catastrophe market is thin."

0:15 Inputs. Drag the date back to mid-2025. "The globe and the news drop anything that was not public yet. The count of incidents falls. That is the same file, filtered by time."

0:30 Press play on Future 1. "This future is an illustrative stand-in, two hand-made scenarios, not a recorded MiroFish run. The caption says so. Pings are simulated. Effects that no market backs are marked game rule. They move this future's hand only."

0:42 Click a ping, then Why. When: the four prices and the trader counts. How dangerous: the report text, labelled simulated, and on this build it is the 24 Oct spike, not this future. Track record: "On 1,186 later questions the market's Brier is 0.085 against a coin at 0.250. A blind model did not beat the market, so its weight is zero. We do not score the countdown date."

0:55 Engine. "This is a replica of MiroFish's own screens, from a saved spike, labelled stand-in. Graph, Split and Workbench. It does not start a live simulation."
