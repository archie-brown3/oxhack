3 · One turn = one in-game month

Rogue AI moves spend: compute

  • Scale up training run → capability ↑, detection ↑
  • Leak or open-source weights → spreads across regions, can't be undone
  • Sandbag evals → hides capability, lowers alarm
  • Acquire cloud and crypto resources → more compute next turn
  • Lobby against regulation → slows the Guardian's moves

Guardian moves spend: political capital

  • Mandatory dangerous-capability evals → reveals sandbagging
  • Compute caps and chip export controls → limits AI's compute budget
  • International treaty → slow, but regions stop spread
  • Kill switch and datacentre audits → can cut a region
  • Public campaign → raises alarm, so more capital next turn

4 · Past data → the future the game starts from

The game's starting world is today's real odds. The red curve is Kalshi's real-money curve for "any company achieves AGI". Those odds set how fast capability grows if nobody acts.

The gap matters: Kalshi gives 81% by 2030 and Manifold's superintelligence market gives 36%. "AGI" there probably means a company announces it (resolution rules not yet read). The game can show both as different finish lines.

Proof it predicts. Start the engine on 1 Jan 2025 with only the data available then. Its forecasts for 2025–26 AI events are scored against what actually happened and against the market at the time, using Manifold's resolved markets and price history. That's the HRT slide.

5 · Where MiroFish fits

Good at

Believable reactions from named actors. They give the news ticker and a measurable "world alarm" to feed the Guardian's budget.

Can't

React to a single click. Nothing can be injected mid-run (scheduled_events is always empty), so each era needs a fresh run.

Blockers

Needs a Zep Cloud key (sign-up) and an LLM key. Runs take minutes (untimed). The frontend is running at localhost:3000.

7 · Risks

Moves are made up. How much a move shifts the odds is our design, not data. Fix: anchor each move to a real market where one exists (e.g. export controls), and label the rest as game rules.
Scope in ~18 h. A game, a backtest, and MiroFish is three projects. The game and engine come first; MiroFish is cached or skipped.
Leakage in the backtest. The LLM may know what happened. Only count markets that resolved after the model's training cutoff.
Taste. "AI kills everyone" can read as a stunt. The backtest slide is what makes it serious.
