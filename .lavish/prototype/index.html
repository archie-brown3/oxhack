Track record

Are the market inputs any good? On AI questions that have already resolved, we score the market price at each checkpoint against a 50% coin and the base rate. Lower Brier is better.

Caveats. Every number here is a fixture until the backtest runs. Questions count only if the model's cutoff plus one month is before the checkpoint; the earlier half picks the panel weight and the later half reports. The clock's date itself can't be backtested, so it is never scored.
