Plague Inc: Evolved, UI inspiration for Doom Clock

These are the 22 Steam store screenshots, grouped into the nine patterns worth borrowing. Each card says what the screenshot shows, what we take, where it lands in Doom Clock, and what we leave out. Click a screenshot to enlarge it. The patterns marked changes a call bear on the globe-style and layout choice in the other Lavish tab.

Source: store.steampowered.com/app/246620 (Ndemic Creations), fetched 24 Oct 2026 with Firecrawl. Screenshots © Ndemic, used here only as internal reference.

The main screen

01The map fills the screen, with four corners of HUDchanges a call

SEEThe map runs edge to edge. The HUD is four small pieces. Top-left is a news ticker in a dropdown. Top-right is the date plus pause/speed. Bottom-left is Disease (DNA points). Bottom-centre is the hovered country or the world (infected/dead plus a bar). Bottom-right is World cure %. Nothing else is on screen while you play.
TAKETheir whole layout is about 10% chrome. It's the strongest evidence for Layout A, full-bleed, and it suggests going further than my schematic did.
OURSTop-left: ticker. Top-right: date, cycle and speed. Bottom-left: political capital. Bottom-centre: hovered region (AI % / oversight %) or the world. Bottom-right: the takeover countdown and the oversight bar. The chart and the move cards slide up from the bottom when you open them, Plague's "Disease" button pattern, rather than staying docked.

02Spread shows as dot density inside each countrychanges a call

SEEInfection isn't a flat country fill. It's a spray of red dots that thickens until the country goes solid. In versus mode (ss18) each plague has its own colour of dots on the same map.
TAKEDensity reads as "spreading" in a way a tint doesn't. That's a point for hex-dot (B). A hybrid also works: solid dark countries (A), with H3 dots lit in proportion to AI influence.
OURSThe AI-influence layer becomes the share of a region's dots lit red. Oversight could be the same dots turning blue, which would replace the circles I drew.

03Routes and the moment of spread

SEEAir and sea routes are thin straight lines with plane and ship icons on them. A new infection is a braided arc that lands on a country, which then lights up (ss05: Greenland to China).
TAKEKeep standing routes faint. Save the bright, animated arc for the moment of spread, so motion always means something just happened.
OURSWeight leaks and cloud compute deals are the Rogue's arcs. They animate once when the move lands, then settle into a faint permanent route. This changes my mock, where every arc animates all the time.

04Bubbles to click on the map

SEERed biohazard pins give DNA when clicked. The hourglass bubble (ss15) marks where cure research starts. Both sit on the map at a place, not in a panel.
TAKEThis is the 10-second hook. A judge sees something to click on the globe before reading any panel.
OURSMiroFish alarm spikes appear as blue bubbles over a region; clicking one gives political capital. A hidden Rogue move is a red "?" pin that a successful eval reveals. Slot: game-design decides the payouts.

05Newsflash cards

SEEA big event gets a centred card with an image, a headline, two lines of copy and an OK button. The game pauses behind it. Smaller news only scrolls in the top-left ticker.
TAKETwo tiers: the ticker for routine news, a card for turning points. Keep the cards rare.
OURSA card only for turning points: a hidden move revealed, a treaty signed, or a new stage of the world-alarm number. It doesn't block play and closes itself after about 4 s. A modal with an OK button stalls a judge.

06Versus: both sides in one header strip, events on the left edge

SEEA top-centre strip puts the two plagues side by side, with each one's numbers and a progress bar. A list down the left edge logs events ("Your starting country has been found", "Opponent starting country found").
TAKE"You vs them" as one thin strip, and the opponent's actions as a short list at the edge of the screen.
OURSHeader: Guardian (oversight %) vs Rogue AI (capability %) in one strip, which replaces the two separate bars. The opponent log is a narrow left-edge list of the last three moves.

Secondary screens

07Evolution history and trend graph, which is our end screen

SEEThe overview has a dated list of every upgrade (Coughing 3-5-2014, Water 1…), a trend graph of the stats over days, and a grid of key stats (start date, location, daily infections).
TAKEThe move history and the line chart, read together, make the replay.
OURSThe end/replay view: a dated move list (both sides) beside the two-line chart, with a marker on the line at each move. This confirms the End view I kept.

08Hex upgrade tree (slot for game-design)

SEEThree tabs (Transmission/Symptoms/Abilities). Each is a hex grid where owned upgrades are lit and the next ones are outlined. The info panel has a name, one line of effect, a cost in DNA, and an Evolve button. Three stat bars run along the bottom.
TAKEIf there's a tree at all: one grid, with the cost on the card and the effect in one line. The three bars along the bottom are worth taking even without a tree.
OURSOnly if game-design adds a tree, and then it opens inside the move tray, not as a separate screen. Without a tree, the five move cards are the whole tree.

09World screen: one status phrase, an ETA and a progress bar

SEE"WORLD: Business as usual" is the headline. The cure card shows "173 days until completion" with an ETA date and a bar. There's also a donut of healthy/infected/dead and country lists.
TAKEThe status phrase, which works like DEFCON stages, and the "N days until" framing. Skip the donut and the lists.
OURSA world-alarm stage label under the countdown ("Business as usual", then "Concerned", "Emergency session", "Lockdown"), fed by MiroFish. We don't build this screen.

Skip

  • Menus and pickers (ss08 plague type, ss09 speedrun, ss11 scenarios, ss01 genotype, ss14 versus splash). A judge has 5–10 minutes, so we start straight on the globe.
  • Skeuomorphic red chrome: bevelled frames, a gradient on every panel, DNA helix wallpaper. Your taste is restrained, so we keep flat translucent panels and one colour per side.
  • The satellite photo map (ss05, ss10). It works for Plague because their red is a dense, saturated dot spray. A flat tint over a photo turns muddy, as option C in the other tab shows.
  • Tiny all-caps labels everywhere. They were designed for 1080p fullscreen. At 1440×900 in a browser, the minimum is 12 px.
