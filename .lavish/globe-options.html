Doom Clock: globe style and Play layout

Two choices. Each globe below is live globe.gl 2.46.2, running on bundled files with no network needed, and all three show the same mock state. Drag to rotate. Land a Guardian move plays the move animation I'm proposing. Rogue move plays the opponent's version, which leaves the camera where it is. The numbers are illustrative.

1 · Globe surface

A · Solid dark countries

recommended
  • Tint reads at a glance. The 46% vs 8% gap is obvious.
  • It's the mockup's look moved onto the globe, so the panels already match.
  • Cheapest to render: one mesh per country.
  • Plainest of the three.

B · Hex-dot countries

  • Looks the most "data", like the GitHub globe.
  • Dots don't hide the arcs and rings.
  • Datacentres are small 3D Monopoly houses, and hyperscale campuses are "hotels". Grey means operating, red compromised, blue audited. Hover one for its name.
  • The tint gradient is harder to read when dots are small.
  • Slowest first render because it tessellates the dots, about 1 s.

C · Night-earth texture

  • Most realistic. City lights suggest compute.
  • Busiest. The lights compete with the red tint and the datacentre dots.
  • Adds a 715 KB texture (fine offline, but it's one more asset).
