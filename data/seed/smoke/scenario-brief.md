# Scenario brief: the Doom Clock world, autumn 2026

This is a labelled simulation for a hackathon game, not a forecast. Frontier AI labs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta, xAI, DeepSeek, Alibaba) are racing to build more capable systems. Governments in the United States, China, the European Union and the United Kingdom are deciding how hard to regulate. Safety researchers warn about loss of control; accelerationists argue that slowing down hands the lead to rivals. The press reports on real AI incidents (deepfakes, chatbot harms, autonomous-system failures) and on the odds that prediction markets give for superintelligence and catastrophe.

The Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists set the 2026 Doomsday Clock at 85 seconds to midnight and named disruptive technologies, including AI, among the reasons.

Actors who react on social media: AI lab leaders, safety researchers, policymakers and regulators, journalists, and members of the public who are worried, excited or indifferent.
